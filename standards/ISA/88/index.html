🏭 ISA-88 (S88)

Batch Process Control Standard | ANSI/ISA-88.00.01

Overview

ISA-88 (S88) is the international standard for batch process control, approved 1995, updated 2010. It defines a design philosophy for describing equipment and procedures—technology agnostic, applicable to automated and manual processes.
